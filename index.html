Quick_AE

Available
Comp presets

Create 4:5 and 9:16 compositions, open composition settings, and switch between 24, 30 and 60 fps.

True comp duplicate

Duplicates a comp and every nested comp inside it, so editing the copy never touches the original.

Align & anchor point

Nine-point align for the selected layers and a nine-point anchor point snap, without moving the layer.

Expression shortcuts

Wiggle, loopOut, inertia bounce and time remap applied to the selected properties in one click.

Bounce & keyframe shortcuts

Easy ease, hold keys, reverse keyframes and a bounce expression dropped straight onto the selection.

Text exploder

Split a text layer into separate layers per character, word or line, ready for staggered animation.

Effects shortcuts

Glow, blur, curves, fill and stroke applied to the selection without opening the Effects panel.

Labels & quick render

Ten one-click label colours for the selection, plus send-to-render-queue on the active comp.

Ps

Quick_PS

Available
New document, one click

1:1, 4:5, 16:9 and 9:16 presets for social, plus custom W × H with a swap button and 72 / 96 / 150 / 300 DPI.

True Duplicate Smart Object

A copy that shares nothing with the original. Embedded objects get independent contents; linked files are duplicated on disk and relinked, so editing one never changes the other.

Smart Object & rasterize

Click to merge the selection into one layer, Shift-click to convert or rasterize each selected layer on its own. Groups flatten to pixels.

Text case

Sentence, lower, UPPER and Capitalized across every selected text layer at once — useful when a client renames a campaign at 2am.
